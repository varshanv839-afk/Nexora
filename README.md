# 🔬 Nexora — ResearchFlow AI

> **Research deeper. Verify smarter. Discover what others miss.**

ResearchFlow AI is an autonomous multi-agent AI research platform designed to transform complex research questions into structured, evidence-backed reports. It uses a collaborative AI research workflow to plan investigations, discover sources, verify claims, detect contradictions, identify research gaps, and synthesize findings.

Instead of functioning like a traditional chatbot, ResearchFlow AI provides an intelligent research workspace where multiple AI agents work together to make research more transparent, organized, and reliable.

## 🚀 Overview

ResearchFlow AI aims to simplify deep research by automating the research process through specialized AI agents. It helps users explore topics, analyze evidence, understand conflicting information, identify missing knowledge, and generate comprehensive research reports.

## ✨ Key Features

- **🤖 Multi-Agent Research System** – Specialized AI agents collaborate to conduct research.
- **🔎 Smart Query Expansion** – Converts research topics into multiple targeted search queries.
- **📚 Source Intelligence** – Evaluates source relevance, recency, diversity, and credibility indicators.
- **🛡️ Evidence Ledger** – Connects individual research claims to supporting evidence.
- **⚡ Contradiction Radar** – Identifies conflicting claims, statistics, and viewpoints across sources.
- **🧩 Research Gap Detector** – Highlights unanswered questions, missing perspectives, and insufficient evidence.
- **🗺️ Knowledge Map** – Visualizes relationships between topics, findings, claims, and sources.
- **📄 AI Report Generation** – Produces structured research reports with findings, analysis, limitations, and references.
- **🕵️ Research Trail** – Maintains a transparent timeline of the research process.
- **🧠 Research Memory** – Reuses relevant findings from previous research while preserving their original context.
- **📂 Document Analysis** – Supports document analysis and comparison with research findings.
- **📊 Research Quality Score** – Evaluates research quality using transparent scoring criteria.

## 🤖 AI Agent Workflow

ResearchFlow AI is designed around four specialized agents:

| Agent | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| 🧠 Planner Agent | Understands the research topic, creates a plan, generates subquestions, and identifies research directions. |
| 🔎 Discovery Agent | Finds relevant research sources, reports, publications, and information. |
| 🛡️ Evidence & Verification Agent | Checks claims, evaluates evidence, identifies contradictions, and flags unsupported information. |
| 📝 Synthesis & Report Agent | Organizes findings, analyzes perspectives, and generates structured research reports. |

### Research Pipeline

**Topic → Planning → Discovery → Evidence Verification → Gap Detection → Synthesis → Report**

## 🔍 Unique Innovations

### 1. Claim–Evidence Ledger
Every major research claim can be linked to supporting sources, evidence excerpts, confidence levels, and verification status.

### 2. Contradiction Radar
Detects disagreements between sources and helps investigate whether conflicts arise from different methodologies, time periods, contexts, or viewpoints.
